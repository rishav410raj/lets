About Us

Speak English Confidently. Build Your Future.

Let's Talk English Class is an English institute in Bhagalpur, Bihar. We help students speak English fluently, understand grammar from basic to advanced, improve communication, and prepare for a better career.

  • Fluent spoken English
  • Grammar from basic to advanced
  • Better communication and public speaking
  • Interview and career preparation
